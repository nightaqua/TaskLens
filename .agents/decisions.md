## How to Use

**Maintainer:** When you approve or reject an agent's proposal (from a PR comment, a flagged item, or a conversation), add a row here. The agent will not re-propose it.

**Agents:** Before adding a task to `backlog.md` or opening a PR, scan this table. If your proposed work matches a `rejected` row, skip it entirely — do not re-propose, do not re-open, do not mention it.
